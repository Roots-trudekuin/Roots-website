You know this

You know where it comes from. And still it happens again.

A silence in the meeting. That one tone in your partner’s voice. Feedback from a colleague. And before you know it you say yes, you snap, you withdraw or you start explaining. Afterwards you know exactly what you would rather have done.

You have done a lot already. Conversations, books, courses. You understand your pattern inside out. Only in the moment itself, that understanding doesn’t help you.

That is not your fault. You were working on a different layer from the one where your pattern is switched on.

Why one to one

Sometimes you don’t want to do this in a group.

Because your pattern is stubborn. Because you would rather not share in a community. Because you want someone to see exactly what happens in you, and adjust straight away.

In the individual programme you have me just for you. We take the time your pattern needs. And I see what you can’t see yourself in that moment.

Challenge or individual?

  • Individual: eight sessions with me, on your pattern and at your pace.
