You know this

You know what you want. And right when it matters, it goes wrong.

The presentation you have been dreading for weeks. The plan that has been ready for months. The choice you keep putting off. Your goal is clear. And still: your body braces itself, your mind goes blank, or suddenly there is something else that needs doing first.

You have tried willpower, planning, giving yourself a talking-to. It helps for a while. Until the moment itself.

That is not about your discipline. An alarm goes off that is faster than your will.

Why willpower doesn’t work

Your body protects you. Just at a moment when you don’t need that protection.

At some point your system learned that moments like this are dangerous. That isn’t in your head, it is in your body. That is why you can’t think it away.

With Havening and hypnosis we work on that layer. Not by pushing past it, but by working with the alarm itself, and with how you do want it.

What you get back

  • Moving towards your goal again
  • Making the choice you have wanted to make for so long

What it involves

Havening and hypnosis

I have worked with both for years. Together they are strong: Havening works with the alarm attached to a moment or memory, hypnosis strengthens how you do want it.

Havening

A method using gentle touch on your arms, hands and face, while your attention is on a particular feeling or moment. Online you do the touch yourself, and I guide you step by step.

Hypnosis

A calm, focused state in which it is easier to feel what you do want. In that state we strengthen the reaction you want to have, so it becomes more natural.

Good to know

Does Havening work online?

Yes. You do the touch yourself, and I guide you. All you need is a quiet place and a laptop, tablet or phone with a camera. With your phone on a stand, you can sit or lie down really comfortably.

Do I have to tell you everything?

No. You tell me what you want to tell me. For Havening it is not necessary to explain everything.

What is the difference with the individual programme?

The 3 sessions are for one goal or one block. Does the same thing keep coming back, in different situations? Then it is a pattern. That is what the individual programme is for: 8 sessions using the full ROOTS steps.
