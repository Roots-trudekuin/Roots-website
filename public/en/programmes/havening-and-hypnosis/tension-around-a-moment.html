What we do

How we work on it

We take that one moment as our starting point. We work with the tension that belongs to it, and with how you do want to experience the moment. So you can stand in it more calmly.

What it involves

Havening and hypnosis

Havening

A method using gentle touch on your arms, hands and face, while your attention is on a particular feeling or moment. Online you do the touch yourself, and I guide you step by step.

Hypnosis

A calm, focused state in which it is easier to feel what you do want. In that state we strengthen the reaction you want to have, so it becomes more natural.

Questions

Good to know

Does Havening work online?

Yes. You do the touch yourself, and I guide you. All you need is a quiet place and a laptop or tablet with a camera.

Do I have to tell you everything?

No. You tell me what you want to tell me. For Havening it is not necessary to explain everything.

What is the difference with the individual programme?

The 3 sessions are for one goal or one block. Does the same thing keep coming back, in different situations? Then it is a pattern. That is what the individual programme is for: 8 sessions using the full ROOTS steps.
