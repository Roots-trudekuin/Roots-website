The woman behind ROOTS

Hi, I’m Trude.

For more than thirty-five years, one question has stayed with me: how do I help people change in a way that gives them lasting influence over it themselves?

That question led me to ROOTS.

Where it began

On the street, I made up the games.

As a child, I was the one on our street with the ideas. The other children joined in and followed my suggestions. I made sure nobody was bullied. Connecting people and taking them along into something new: that was there from early on.

Later I studied health sciences. I became a trainer of job-search groups, an adviser and a recruiter, and guided people in choosing careers and studies. Then came coaching, hypnotherapy and Havening. Thousands of conversations, always getting closer to the core. What is going on inside people? What holds them back? And how do they set their potential in motion?

The question I couldn’t let go of

Leaving happy. And two weeks later, everything was back.

In my work, I saw something I could never let go of. People came in feeling awful and seeing no way forward. They left happy. And two weeks later, at the next appointment, all their old patterns were back.

“How do I help people change in a way that gives them lasting influence over it themselves?”

Not dependent on me. Not until the next appointment. But in a way they can do themselves, later on too.

Myself first

My own patterns were my first material for investigation.

I always asked three questions. How does this work? What can I influence? And how can I use that influence to achieve what I want? I asked those questions about my own life too.

That meant my patterns were not just things I wanted to be free of. They became material for investigation. Whatever I discovered, I first applied to myself. That meant I knew exactly why I was doing something in my practice, what I was working on and what outcome I was aiming for.

In 2014 I was at my lowest point. Ill from the stress after my business went bankrupt, everything lost, and no doctor or psychologist who knew what to do. That’s when I decided: I would rather lose everything than carry on like this. If no one can help me heal, I’ll do it myself. That’s where what would later be called ROOTS began.

That is how I freed myself from patterns that kept leading me into the same pitfalls. And created what mattered most to me: using my full potential for greater awareness, love and creativity.

How ROOTS grew

From separate insights to a method.

I kept exploring. My own experiences, and what science was discovering about change. My curiosity lies in connecting different perspectives. When I begin to see how things fit together, something happens that I deeply enjoy: what once seemed complicated becomes manageable. And therefore something you can influence.

That is how ROOTS grew. A method that doesn’t separate body and mind, and keeps your story and your neurobiology together. With which you switch off your pattern at the source, and then learn to adjust it yourself.

The most intimate part of yourself, how you feel and how you think, should belong to you.

Why ROOTS exists

Becoming who you could have been. Before you look back in twenty years and think: if only I had known this sooner.

No guru. No lifelong guidance. A guide who teaches you the craft, so you can adjust your patterns yourself. Later on too. Without me.
